ASPIRATIONAL LIVING

PERFECT HAPPINESS & ROOFTOP LIVING

"Experience an unmatched blend of luxury, calm, and convenience in the heart of Govind Nagar with bespoke limited edition residences designed for complete family wellness."

The Avenue Bliss introduces a refined architectural milestone featuring thoughtfully planned 2 & 3 BHK elevated homes, along with generous 4 BHK customizable configurations. Situated on a 12 meter wide proposed road behind Prakash Petrol Pump in Govind Nagar, this signature development delivers pristine cross ventilation, abundant natural light, and unmatched urban connectivity.

With possession ready in 3 months, homebuyers can transition seamlessly into an exclusive community equipped with complete lifestyle conveniences, dual high speed elevators, CCTV monitored entrance corridors, secure video door access, and dedicated rooftop recreational zones.
