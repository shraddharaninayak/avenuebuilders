Architectural Vision

Four Tiers of Celebrity-Like Lifestyle

Urbania 3 & 4 BHK residences are created for those who never compromise. A revolution in high-end living taking your 'joy of living' quotient to unprecedented heights.

A joint venture of The Avenue Builders & Developers and Fortune, Urbania introduces a distinctive multi-tiered lifestyle philosophy designed to offer dedicated spaces for private living, community wellness, social recreation, and rooftop relaxation.

“An elite abode that reflects the best of you. Let the fortune smile where you reside.”

Each tower rises 20 storeys into the Nashik skyline with expansive architectural balconies, sleek glass curtain elevator cores, and grand entrance monuments. Offering 3 and 4 BHK sky residences as well as palatial 5 and 6 BHK full-floor penthouses, Urbania provides complete acoustic privacy with just two apartments per floor.

Residents enjoy private elevator foyers, dedicated stilt and podium car parking, automated video door phones, and 24x7 gated security across the entire landscaped campus.
