Welcome to Viraj Avenue

Viraj Avenue is an upcoming commercial landmark in Nashik, thoughtfully engineered to offer an exceptional blend of Modern Office Spaces, Showrooms, Shops, a Banquet Hall, and a Hospital.

Strategically located opposite Bali Mandir, Panchavati Annex, this multi-tier development caters to a complete spectrum of business, corporate, and healthcare needs with state-of-the-art infrastructure and elevated facilities.

Fronting a massive 60.00 Meter Wide D.P. Road with seamless access to Mumbai-Agra Highway, Viraj Avenue commands supreme visibility and effortless customer ingress.

Featuring dedicated passenger lifts for each wing, double-height shop mezzanines, a grand 342.56 Sq.M landscaped open court, and a clubhouse, Viraj Avenue sets a new benchmark in commercial excellence.

Location & Route Connectivity

Opp. Bali Mandir, Panchavati Annex

Positioned along the vibrant Mumbai-Agra Highway corridor in Panchavati Annex, Viraj Avenue provides unmatched arterial connectivity across Nashik's residential, commercial, and transit hubs.

  • Bali Maharaj Mandir
    Directly opposite the project site for instant civic recognition.
  • Mumbai-Agra National Highway
    Seamless vehicular transit toward Mumbai, Dhule, and Nashik city centre.
  • Apollo Hospital & KK Wagh College
    Major healthcare and premier educational institutions within quick reach.
  • DMart, Dhatrak Phata & Amrutdham
    High-density retail markets and prominent transit intersections nearby.
