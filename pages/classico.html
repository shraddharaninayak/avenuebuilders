Roman Architecture & Modern Intelligence

The Avenue Builders & Developers proudly presents THE CLASSICO – Premium 4 BHK Living, a timeless residence inspired by the grandeur of Roman architecture. Elegant facades, graceful columns, refined symmetry, and superior craftsmanship come together to create an address of unmatched prestige.

Designed with the singular exclusivity of One Floor, One Royal Family, THE CLASSICO blends classical elegance with modern comforts, smart security, and future-ready amenities for a truly elevated lifestyle.

More than a home, THE CLASSICO is a statement of sophistication, heritage & innovation — where the glory of the past meets the brilliance of tomorrow.

Since 2019, we have been shaping exceptional residential and commercial destinations with visionary planning, superior craftsmanship & enduring value across Nashik.

Engineering Excellence

Crafted With Expertise

Uncompromising quality in every detail. Built with premium materials, branded fittings, and advanced structural standards.

RCC Structure

Earthquake-resistant reinforced cement concrete frame structure designed to withstand seismic forces.

Masonry & Plaster

High-insulation AAC block work with smooth interior gypsum plaster finish for immaculate wall surfaces.

Flooring

Premium Large Format Polished Glazed Vitrified Tiles (PGVT) in living, dining, kitchen, and all bedrooms.

Doors & Smart Locks

Decorative main door with both-side natural wood veneer. Laminated internal doors and door frames. Smart digital door locks.

Windows

High quality sound-insulating UPVC / anodised aluminium sliding windows with clear glass panels.

Bathrooms & Lighting

Proximity sensor-based smart lighting with designer false ceiling in all bathrooms. Premium designer wall tiles upto lintel level.

Kitchen & Store

Granite / composite marble kitchen platform with stainless steel sink, ceramic dado tiles, and dedicated utility wash yard.

Electrification

Smart light control system in flat areas. 3-phase power connection with concealed ISI copper wiring, modular switches, and MCB panel.

Sanitary & CP Fittings

Wall-hung EWCs with concealed flush valves from Hindware / Kohler / Grohe or equivalent with composite marble counter basins.

Balconies & Railings

Anti-skid rustic ceramic tiles on balconies paired with MS / aluminium / toughened safety glass railings.

Lobby & Common Areas

Designer entrance lobby with luxury seating arrangements and imported Italian marble finishes in all common elevator lobbies.

Eco & Water Systems

Integrated rainwater harvesting system, automated water level sensors for overhead tank, and running hot water provisions.
