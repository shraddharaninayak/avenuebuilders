Prime Location & Connectivity

  • Located at the prestigious landmark OLD GANGAPUR NAKA, in the bustling heart of Nashik.
  • Minutes from CBS (Central Bus Stand) and the vibrant College Road shopping district.
  • Dual road corner accessibility: 15.00 Meter main arterial road and 9.00 Meter wide secondary road.
  • Close proximity to Dongre Vasatigruh Ground, Pramod Mahajan Garden, and Chopada Lawns.
  • Immediate walking access to Vihar Misal, Jankalyan Blood Bank, and premier financial institutes.
  • Unrivalled pedestrian footfall and high-visibility vehicular frontage for retail showrooms.
  • Convenient, smooth connectivity to Gangapur Road, Canada Corner, and Mumbai-Agra Highway.

Commercial Infrastructure

  • Distinctive glass curtain wall facade delivering an imposing, modern corporate identity.
  • Expansive ground and first floor retail showrooms with maximized glazed display frontage.
  • Independent Wing A and Wing B entrance lobbies with dedicated high-speed automatic elevators.
  • Spacious 6-foot wide central distribution corridors ensuring effortless visitor circulation.
  • Corporate suites on 2nd to 7th floors engineered with private balconies and uninhibited natural light.
  • Dedicated ramp access to multi-level basement parking plus ample surface vehicular parking.
  • Advanced fire-fighting network, electrical transformer backup, and 24x7 security surveillance infrastructure.
